Exposed & trapped humanity
Billions in unprecedented heat (≥29 °C), hot + polluted air, and the survivability-risk zone.

Centuries, not decades

Warming stops only at net-zero CO₂ and then holds. The conditions of the 2090s are a floor for the centuries that follow — not a peak. Under a pessimistic reading the survivability-risk zone expands and deepens into a slow polycrisis apocalypse: cascading heat, pollution, water stress, crop failure and migration pressure compounding over centuries to millennia, with sea-level rise locked in for millennia.

The long-run floor · 2025–2500
Median human temperature and survivability-risk-zone share, held at 2100 levels.
Polycrisis apocalypse — pessimistic tail
In the pessimistic projection the late-century majority is trapped in regions where heat, polluted air, water stress and weak institutions compound. Feedback loops — heat → crop failure → migration → conflict → failed adaptation → more emissions from emergency cooling and burning biomass — can lock in decline over centuries. The report's central estimate keeps the trap at a large minority (~1.7 billion, ≈17%); the pessimistic tail makes it the median human condition.
The upside engine
Cheap solar (−89% module cost since 2010), AI-accelerated diffusion, girls' education and clean electricity are the levers that decide whether the trap shrinks or becomes the median. Technology now reaches poor countries in years, not generations.
